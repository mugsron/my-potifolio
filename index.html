My interest in computers started in Primary Four. It’s been growing ever since.

When I got my first computer in Senior Three, theory became practice. I explored how software works, installed Windows, and learned to diagnose problems by experimenting with my own machine.

Soon, I was helping others—including troubleshooting computers at my church. I began learning programming during my Senior Four vacation, turning that same curiosity into the ability to build things.

Today, I combine practical experience with ongoing study. I use research, persistence, and AI tools to solve problems, work efficiently, and keep learning.
